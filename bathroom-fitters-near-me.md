Searching "bathroom fitters near me" usually means a full refit, a shower or wet room, or retiling. The checks are similar for all three.

Short answer: ask two or three local bathroom fitters to look at the job, get each price in writing for the same scope (strip-out, plumbing, electrics, tiling, waste removal, making good), and ask who handles the electrics. Electrical work near a bath or shower, and any new circuit such as for an electric shower, is notifiable in England under Part P, so it should be done by a registered electrician who can certify it. Anything to do with gas, such as moving a boiler or gas pipe, needs a Gas Safe registered engineer.

## First, is anything unsafe right now?

- **Water near electrics.** If a leak is dripping onto a light, fan or shower, or water is coming through a ceiling near a fitting, keep away from it. Switch off at the fuse box only if you can reach it with dry hands and a dry floor, then turn off the water at the stopcock.
- **Gas smell.** Open windows, don't use switches or flames, and call the free National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999.
- **Shower that tingles or scorch marks on the pull cord or isolator.** Stop using it and get an electrician out before the refit starts.

## What should I ask a bathroom fitter before I book?

1. **What's in the price.** Taking out the old suite, plumbing, tiling, flooring, plastering, and taking the waste away. Ask what isn't included.
2. **Who does the electrics.** Lights, an extractor fan, a shaver socket or a new electric shower all need an electrician. A registered electrician (for example NICEIC or NAPIT) can self-certify notifiable work and give you a certificate.
3. **Who does any gas work.** Only a Gas Safe registered engineer can work on gas. Check them on the Gas Safe Register.
4. **How the room is ventilated.** Bathrooms need a way to get steam out. An extractor fan is the usual answer.
5. **Waterproofing.** For a wet room or a walk-in shower, ask how they will tank (waterproof) the floor and walls.
6. **Waste.** If they take the old bathroom away, they should hold a waste carrier licence.
7. **Recent photos and reviews** of bathrooms like yours.

## FAQ

**Can a bathroom fitter wire in my electric shower?**
Only if they are a qualified electrician, or bring one in. A new shower circuit is notifiable work.

**Should I get more than one price?**
Yes. Two or three written prices for the same scope make it easy to compare.

**Do I need to empty the bathroom first?**
Usually yes. Ask the fitter when the water will be off and whether you'll have a working toilet during the job.
