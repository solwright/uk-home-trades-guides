## What do you need to join?

- Your own MCS certification for solar PV
- Public liability insurance
- The working areas and days you want jobs in

If you work under someone else's MCS, you can't take solar jobs on the programme until you hold your own. EV charger jobs are different and are live now.

## How does a job work?

1. The customer buys the kit from AIZO Quality Heating.
2. They answer questions about the install and pay a booking fee.
3. The job goes to the closest installers first. First to accept gets it.
4. You fit it. If something on site wasn't in the estimate, you agree the extra with the customer.
5. The customer pays you once the job is done, through the Tradehand app or directly.

The amount on the offer is the full amount you receive. It's a referral. You own the job.

## FAQ

**Do I pay for leads?**
No. You never pay to get a job.

**Who handles MCS paperwork?**
You do, as the MCS installer on the job.

**Do I have to take every job?**
No. You see the area, the kit, the photos and your pay first. Skip anything that doesn't suit you.

**How many jobs will I get?**
Nobody can promise that. Jobs depend on where AIZO customers buy.
