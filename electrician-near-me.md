Searching "electrician near me" can mean anything from a new socket to a burning smell. Make it safe first, then pick the electrician.

Short answer: if you can smell burning, see sparks or scorch marks, or something feels warm or tingles, stop using it and switch it off at the fuse box only if you can do that safely with dry hands. If there is smoke or fire, get out and call 999. Then compare a few local electricians, check they are registered with a scheme such as NICEIC or NAPIT, and get the price in writing before work starts.

## What should I do before the electrician arrives?

1. **Stop using anything that smells hot, buzzes or sparks.** Unplug it only if the plug and socket are cool and dry.
2. **Switch off at the fuse box if it's safe.** Dry hands, dry floor, and don't touch anything that is scorched or wet.
3. **Keep water away from electrics.** If a leak is near sockets, lights or the fuse box, keep clear and call an electrician.
4. **Never open up the fuse box or a socket yourself.** Electrical work in a home has to meet Part P of the Building Regulations in England and Wales.
5. **Power cut on your street?** Call 105, the free power cut line, before booking anyone.

## How do I find a good electrician near me?

- **Check registration.** Scheme members such as NICEIC or NAPIT can self-certify notifiable work, so you get the right paperwork.
- **Match the job.** EV chargers, EICRs, consumer unit replacements and fault finding are the most common jobs. Pick someone who lists your job.
- **Read recent reviews** and look for detail, not just stars.
- **Get the price in writing** and ask what happens if they find something extra.
- **Ask for the certificate** at the end, such as an Electrical Installation Certificate or a Minor Works certificate.

## FAQ

**Can I do small electrical jobs myself?**
Swapping a like-for-like light fitting is sometimes allowed, but anything in a bathroom, a new circuit or fuse box work should go to a registered electrician. If in doubt, don't.

**How fast can an electrician come out?**
It depends on the area and the job. For anything unsafe, say so when you call so they can prioritise it.

**What is an EICR?**
An Electrical Installation Condition Report is a safety check of your wiring. Landlords in England need one at least every five years.
