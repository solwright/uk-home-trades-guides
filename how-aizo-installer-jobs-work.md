## How does the job work?

1. The customer buys the kit from AIZO Quality Heating.
2. They book the fitting.
3. You fit it.
4. They pay you after the job.

Tradehand refers the booking. You own the install you accept. This is referral fit work, not a Tradehand day-rate crew. The kit should be on site before you arrive.

## How do I get paid?

The customer pays a booking fee. The amount on your offer is the full amount you receive. They pay you after the job, through the Tradehand app or directly. You never pay a lead fee.

## What should I check before I accept?

- Kit make and model you are happy to fit
- Photos and customer answers look clear enough
- Pay on the offer is clear
- Job is inside your areas and days
- You have the competence and insurance for that install type
- If MCS applies, you handle MCS yourself

Closest installers see the job first. First to accept wins. Skip freely.

Harder EV jobs pay more. Old fuse board, long cable run, trench, missing earth bonding, and London or South East uplift are listed on the live page and on each offer before you accept. Always trust the live page over this guide if the numbers move.

## FAQ

**Is this buying leads?**
No. The customer already bought the kit. You get a referred fit job.

**Will I get a set number of jobs a week?**
No promise. Jobs only appear where AIZO customers buy in the areas you set.

**Who does MCS?**
You do, when the work needs it. Tradehand refers the job. It does not take over your tickets.

**What does pay cover?**
Labour, standard cable and parts, the certificate and the notifications (per the live programme FAQ).

**Do I have to take every job?**
No. You see the area, the kit, the customer's photos and answers, and your pay before you accept.
