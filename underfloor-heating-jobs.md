## Wet or electric: who can take which?

| Type on the live page | Who it's for | From |
|------|------|------|
| Underfloor heating (wet systems, for example ThermRite panels and Quality Heating single room or multi zone kits) | Heating engineers | £877.50, depending on the job |
| Electric underfloor heating (mats and loose cable, for example ThermoSphere, ThermRite and Warmup) | Electricians | £310.50, depending on the job |

For either one you need public liability insurance and the working areas and days you want jobs in.

## How much do underfloor heating jobs pay?

Wet systems start at £877.50 and go up to £1,525.50 depending on the product, with bigger floor areas paying more on top. Electric systems start at £310.50, and if there's no suitable circuit for the mat and thermostat, the new circuit adds £216.

The live pages say the pay covers your labour, standard cable and parts, the certificate and the notifications. Every offer shows your full pay before you accept, so check the page before you rely on any number, as rates get tuned from installer feedback.

## How does a job work?

1. The customer buys the kit from AIZO Quality Heating, and it's on site before you arrive.
2. They answer questions about the install and pay a booking fee.
3. The job goes to the closest installers first. First to accept gets it.
4. You fit it. If something on site wasn't in the estimate, you agree the extra with the customer.
5. The customer pays you once the job's done, through the Tradehand app or directly.

It's a referral. The job is yours.

No. You see the job and your pay first and only take the ones you want.

**How many jobs will I get?**
Nobody can promise that. Jobs depend on where AIZO customers buy.
