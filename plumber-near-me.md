Searching "plumber near me" usually means water is going somewhere it shouldn't. Sort the water first, then pick the plumber.

Short answer: turn off your stopcock if water is leaking, keep water away from electrics, and if you smell gas leave and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999. Then compare a few local plumbers, check they cover your postcode and the job type, and get the price in writing before work starts.

## What should I do before the plumber arrives?

1. **Turn off the water.** The stopcock is usually under the kitchen sink, near the front door, or where the water pipe comes into the house.
2. **Keep away from wet electrics.** If water is near sockets, lights or the fuse box, don't touch them. Only switch off at the fuse box if you can reach it with dry hands and dry floor.
3. **Open taps to drain the system** once the stopcock is off. This empties the pipes and slows the leak.
4. **Gas smell?** Leave, don't use switches, and call 0800 111 999. Boiler or gas work needs a Gas Safe registered engineer, not just a plumber.
5. **Take photos.** They help the plumber price the job and help with any insurance claim.

## How do I compare plumbers near me?

- **Coverage:** do they work in your postcode and do this job type (leak, toilet, tap, boiler)?
- **Gas jobs:** check the Gas Safe register for anything touching gas.
- **Reviews:** read recent ones, not just the star rating.
- **Price:** ask for the price, or how they charge, before they start.
- **Call-out:** ask if there's an extra charge for evenings or weekends.

## How much does a plumber cost?

It depends on the job. Tradehand's plumber page puts an emergency burst pipe repair at typically £100 to £500. Simple jobs like a dripping tap cost less. Boilers and bathroom refits cost more.

## FAQ

**What counts as a plumbing emergency?**
A burst pipe, water coming through a ceiling, no water at all, or a leak near electrics. Turn the water off first.

**Can a plumber fix my boiler?**
Only if they're Gas Safe registered for that work. Check before they start.

**Should I pay upfront?**
Agree the price and payment terms before work starts, and keep it in writing.
