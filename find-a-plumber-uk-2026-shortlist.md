## What finding a plumber actually is (and what it is not)

<div markdown="0"><p>Finding a plumber means finding someone local and available, getting a fair price, and confirming they are competent for the work. Plumbing jobs often feel urgent, which is when people post in a hurry and end up with a flood of calls.</p>
<p>It is not the same as finding a gas engineer. Boilers, gas hobs and gas pipework need a Gas Safe registered engineer. Many plumbers are both, but check.</p>
<p>It is also not an emergency service. For a burst pipe, turn off the stopcock and call an emergency plumber directly. If you smell gas, call the gas emergency line. Online quote forms are for planned and routine work: dripping taps, leaks under the sink, toilet repairs and bathroom work.</p>
<p>For bathroom fits and new pipework, a WaterSafe approved plumber can certify the work meets the Water Fittings Regulations.</p></div>
