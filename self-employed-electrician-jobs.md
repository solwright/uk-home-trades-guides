## What do I need?

- For EV jobs: an EV charging qualification, public liability insurance, and the areas and days you want to work. Some chargers also need Part P, NICEIC or NAPIT registration and OZEV authorised installer status.
- For showers, radiators and electric underfloor heating: to be an electrician, public liability insurance, and your areas and days.

## How much does it pay?

The from figures are above. Extra work pays extra, and the offer shows it before you accept. A few examples from the live pages:

The live pages say the pay covers your labour, standard cable and parts, the certificate and the notifications. Rates get tuned from installer feedback, so check the page before you rely on a number.

## How does a job work?

1. The customer buys the kit from AIZO Quality Heating, and it's on site before you arrive.
2. They answer questions about the install and pay a booking fee.
3. The job goes to the closest installers first. First to accept gets it.
4. You do the install. Anything on site that wasn't in the estimate, you agree with the customer.
5. The customer pays you once the job's done, through the Tradehand app or directly.

It's a referral, not a job with a firm. The customer is yours and you stay self employed.
