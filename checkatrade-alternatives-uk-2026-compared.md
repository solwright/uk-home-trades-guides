## What a Checkatrade alternative actually is (and what it is not)

<div markdown="0"><p>People look for Checkatrade alternatives for different reasons: they want a price faster, fewer calls, a different set of trades, or simply a second opinion. A good alternative does at least one of those better.</p>
<p>A Checkatrade alternative is not automatically a checked directory. Some services on this list check trades in detail, some only collect reviews, and some only connect you. The comparison table shows which is which, from each service's own pages.</p>
<p>It is also not a replacement for checking registration. For gas work, check the Gas Safe Register. For notifiable electrical work, check the Registered Competent Person Electrical register. For water fittings, look for a WaterSafe approved plumber.</p>
<p>The simplest approach is to use one service to find and price the job, and a register to confirm the trade.</p></div>

Your details stay with the one business that takes the job rather than going to a shared lead list. Tradehand lists 7,858 electricians and 998 plumbers across the United Kingdom on its UK pages as read on 14 October 2026.</p>
<p>Where it is weaker: a claimed Tradehand listing is verified by email, and listings are built from public registers. That is not the same as a multi-step background check programme, so you still check scheme membership yourself for notifiable or regulated work. It is also for planned and routine jobs, not emergencies.</p>
<p><strong>Best suited for:</strong> homeowners and renters who want a price before anyone visits and do not want their phone number passed round. It suits small and medium jobs where the scope is easy to describe.</p><h3>2. Which? Trusted Traders, best for trades endorsed against a trading standards assessment</h3><p>Which? Trusted Traders lists traders who have passed an assessment set by its trading standards professionals. Every customer review on the site is checked by its moderators.</p>
<p>Where it is weaker: coverage depends on which traders have applied and passed, so in some areas and trades there are few options. There is no instant price.</p>
<p><strong>Best suited for:</strong> homeowners who want an independent consumer brand's endorsement before they choose, especially for larger home improvement jobs.</p><h3>3. MyBuilder, best for posting a job and choosing from trades who are interested</h3><p>MyBuilder works the other way round from a directory. You post the job for free, MyBuilder alerts relevant tradespeople, and those who are interested express interest. You then start chats with the ones you like.</p>
<p>Its published process: contact details are shared only when you say so, interested trades send you a quote or estimated cost, and you can check profiles, work history and reviews before choosing. Customers review completed jobs. On the trade side, MyBuilder charges a fee per job a trade responds to, shown before they respond.</p>
<p>Where it is weaker: you wait for interest rather than getting a price straight away, and the quotes you see depend on which trades respond.</p>
<p><strong>Best suited for:</strong> homeowners with a defined project who are happy to post once and compare a few responses. It works well for building and renovation work.</p><h3>4. Rated People, best for comparing up to 3 quotes from rated trades</h3><p>Rated People is a job-posting site now part of the Checkatrade Group. You fill in a request with a description, photos or videos, and tradespeople send quotes.</p>
<p>Its published numbers: free to post with no hidden fees, every tradesperson is rated and reviewed, and you get up to 3 quotes to compare.</p>
<p>Where it is weaker: the trades who quote are the ones who choose to respond, and you will not see a price until they do.</p>
<p><strong>Best suited for:</strong> homeowners who want a small number of quotes to compare without calling round. Add photos to get closer estimates.</p><h3>5. MyJobQuote, best for a quick request that up to 3 local trades respond to</h3><p>MyJobQuote is a free online connection service. You submit a short request and up to 3 local trades get in touch to quote for the work.</p>
<p>Its published numbers: completely free, no obligation, and up to 3 quotes to compare.</p>
<p>Where it is weaker: up to 3 businesses contact you, so expect calls. There is no price before they do.</p>
<p><strong>Best suited for:</strong> homeowners who are happy to take a few calls in exchange for a few quotes on a simple job.</p><h3>6. Bark, best for services beyond the core trades</h3><p>Bark is a national marketplace covering a wide range of services, not just trades. You answer a few questions about the job and Bark searches its professionals for matches.</p>
<p>Its published process: Bark says it searches thousands of professionals, and professionals are only given your details once they have sent you a quote. On the trade side, Bark sells credits that professionals spend to respond to leads.</p>
<p>Where it is weaker: it is broad rather than trade-specialist, and it does not publish trade-specific checks on the pages we read.</p>
<p><strong>Best suited for:</strong> homeowners who need something outside the usual trades, or who want to compare several types of provider in one place.</p></div>

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best alternative to Checkatrade?**  
For a free instant price before anyone visits, Tradehand. For an independent endorsement, Which? Trusted Traders. For posting a job and comparing responses, MyBuilder or Rated People.

**Is Rated People owned by Checkatrade?**  
Rated People says on its own site that it is now part of the Checkatrade Group.

**How do I find a tradesperson without Checkatrade?**  
Get an instant quote on Tradehand, search by trade and area, or post a job on MyBuilder or Rated People. Then check the trade on the relevant register before work starts.

**What checks does Checkatrade do that alternatives do not?**  
Checkatrade publishes up to 12 checks, including ID, address, qualifications, CCJs, directors and finances, and repeats them in the background. Tradehand verifies claimed listings by email and builds listings from public registers, so check scheme membership yourself.

**How much do Checkatrade alternatives cost homeowners?**  
Tradehand, MyBuilder, Rated People and MyJobQuote all say they are free for homeowners. You pay the trade for the work.

**How do I avoid lots of calls after asking for quotes?**  
Use a service that limits who gets your details. On Tradehand only the business that takes the job contacts you; Rated People and MyJobQuote cap it at up to 3.
