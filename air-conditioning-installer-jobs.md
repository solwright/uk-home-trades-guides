Search air conditioning installer jobs and you mostly get salaried HVAC roles. If you already fit air con for yourself and just want more installs without paying for leads, this is a different route.

Short answer: the AIZO Quality Heating installer programme refers sold air con installs to local fitters who have joined. The customer buys the unit. You fit it. The customer pays you once the job is done. Air conditioning installation on the programme starts from £153, depending on the job.

Being straight about timing: the live air con page says AIZO customers can't book air con fitting yet. When they can, the jobs go to fitters who have already joined. So think of this as getting set up early, not a promise of work next week.

## What do you need to join?

- REFCOM registration
- Public liability insurance
- The working areas and days you want jobs in

## How much do air con jobs pay?

From £153 a job, and up to £954 depending on the product. The £153 end is the simpler all-in-one units. Split systems sit higher. Extra work adds to it, and every offer shows your full pay before you accept.

| Pay by product on the live page | From |
|------|------|
| All-in-one units (for example Panasonic Rac Solo) | £153 |
| Compact wall units (for example Panasonic Tz Super Compact) | £423 |
| Split and multi split systems (Panasonic, Worcester Bosch, Ariston) | £819 |
| Worcester Bosch Climate split with remote | £954 |

Two of the extras listed on the page: a pipe run of 4 to 7 metres between the indoor and outdoor units adds £180, and a 4.2 kW unit adds £135.

The live page says the pay covers your labour, standard cable and parts, the certificate and the notifications. Check the page before you rely on any number, as rates get tuned from installer feedback.

## How does a job work?

1. The customer buys the unit from AIZO Quality Heating, and it's on site before you arrive.
2. They answer questions about the install and pay a booking fee.
3. The job goes to the closest fitters first. First to accept gets it.
4. You fit it. If something on site wasn't in the estimate, you agree the extra with the customer.
5. The customer pays you once the job is done, through the Tradehand app or directly.

It's a referral. The job is yours.

## FAQ

**Do I pay for leads?**
No. You never pay to get a job.

**Do I have to take every job?**
No. You see the job and your pay first and only take the ones you want.

**How many jobs will I get?**
Nobody can promise that. Jobs depend on where AIZO customers buy.

**I'm also an electrician. Anything live now?**
EV charger installation is live on the programme, from £594 depending on the job, if you hold an EV charger qualification.
